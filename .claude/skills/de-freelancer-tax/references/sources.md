Last cross-check: 2026-10-03. Primary sites were blocked by the cloud session proxy, so figures marked (S) come from secondary sources only.

## Primary (use when reachable)
- Statutes: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de (EStG, UStG, UStDV, GewStG, AO, StBerG)
- Ministry of Finance, BMF letters and UStAE: https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de
- Federal Fiscal Court: https://www.bundesfinanzhof.de
- Filing portal: https://www.elster.de

## Known traps
- Gewinnfreibetrag searches return Austrian law (WKO, USP, Sparkasse.at). Germany has no equivalent for EÜR taxpayers.
- Older articles quote EUR 22,000 / EUR 50,000 gross for Kleinunternehmer (pre-2025).
- Re-verify every year-specific figure in January.
