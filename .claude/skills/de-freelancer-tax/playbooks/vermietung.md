# Rental income (Vermietung und Verpachtung, § 21 EStG)

**Taxed as** income from letting real estate; profit = rent minus costs (Überschuss der Einnahmen über die Werbungskosten). Deductible: interest, maintenance, management, insurance, property tax, depreciation.

**Building depreciation (§ 7 Abs. 4 EStG, statute checked).** 3% p.a. for residential buildings completed after 31 Dec 2022; 2% for completed 1925-2022; 2.5% before 1925. Special declining-balance rule for new residential buildings (§ 7 Abs. 5a: 5%), only for certain construction dates - check eligibility.

**Traps.** Rent below 50% of market rent splits the costs (§ 21 Abs. 2); acquisition-related maintenance within 3 years of purchase over 15% of building cost is treated as acquisition cost (§ 6 Abs. 1 Nr. 1a); sale within 10 years -> taxable gain (§ 23 Abs. 1 Nr. 1, statute checked). VAT: residential letting is normally VAT-exempt (§ 4 Nr. 12 UStG), so no input VAT.

**Form.** Anlage V; for several owners a declaration for separate and uniform determination (gesonderte und einheitliche Feststellung).
