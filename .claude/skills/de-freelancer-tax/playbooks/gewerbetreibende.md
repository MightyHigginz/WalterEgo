# Gewerbetreibende (trade business, sole trader or partnership)

**Registration.** Gewerbeanmeldung at the municipal trade office, then Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung; membership of IHK/HWK may follow.

**Trade tax (GewStG, statute checked).** Gewerbeertrag = profit adjusted by add-backs/deductions (§§ 8, 9 GewStG), rounded down to 100 EUR, minus allowance EUR 24,500 for natural persons and partnerships (§ 11 Abs. 1), x rate 3.5% (§ 11 Abs. 2) = Messbetrag x local rate (Hebesatz). Credit against income tax: 4.0 x Messbetrag (§ 35 EStG), limited by the part of income tax attributable to the trade income. Result: with Hebesatz ~400% the trade tax is mostly neutralised for sole traders; above that there is a residual cost.

**Accounting.** EÜR unless the Finanzamt notifies bookkeeping duty: turnover > EUR 800,000 or profit > EUR 80,000 per year (§ 141 AO, statute checked) -> then double-entry bookkeeping (Bilanz). Commercial-law duty (HGB) applies to registered merchants beyond small-trader thresholds.

**VAT.** Soll-Versteuerung by default; Ist-Versteuerung up to EUR 800,000 prior-year turnover (§ 20 Nr. 1 UStG). Kleinunternehmer possible.

**Planning.** IAB § 7g, degressive AfA 30%, e-vehicle write-off, GWG/Sammelposten, Basisrente, § 35 credit awareness (don't double-count), form choice (sole trader vs. GmbH) when profit passes the level where the progressive tariff exceeds ~30% (see gmbh-ug.md), Verlustvortrag (§ 10d).

**Risks.** Cash-register rules (KassenSichV) for cash businesses; Gewerbeuntersagung for unreliability; hobby/loss rules (Liebhaberei) if persistent losses; fines for late filing (§ 152 AO).
