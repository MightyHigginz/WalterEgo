# Annual calendar (statute-based)

| Date | What | Basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10th of month (or quarter-end +10) | VAT advance return and payment; +1 month with Dauerfristverlängerung | § 18 UStG, UStDV |
| 10 Mar / 10 Jun / 10 Sep / 10 Dec | Income tax advance payments | § 37 EStG |
| 31 Jul (following year) | Income tax return deadline, self-prepared | § 149 Abs. 2 AO |
| End of Feb (2nd following year) | Deadline with a Steuerberater | § 149 Abs. 3 AO |
| After 14 months | Late-filing surcharge becomes mandatory | § 152 Abs. 2 AO |
| 31 Dec | Last day for purchases (AfA/§ 7g), prepayments (§ 11), Basisrente contributions for the year | § 7, § 7g, § 10, § 11 EStG |
| End Feb (2nd following year) | Last day to waive Kleinunternehmer status for a year | § 19 Abs. 3 UStG |
| Jan | Re-check yearly figures: Grundfreibetrag, Basisrente cap, thresholds | various |
