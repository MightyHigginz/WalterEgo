# Kleinunternehmer (§ 19 UStG)

**Statute checked.** Turnover (Gesamtumsatz, net, cash basis) up to EUR 25,000 in the prior year and up to EUR 100,000 in the current year -> VAT-exempt (§ 19 Abs. 1). Waiver (Verzicht): until end of Feb of the 2nd following year, binds 5 years (Abs. 3). Crossing EUR 100,000 ends the status immediately.

**Consequences.** No VAT on invoices, no input VAT (Vorsteuer) recovery, no advance VAT returns or annual VAT return (§ 19 Abs. 1 S. 2 UStG disapplies § 18 Abs. 1-4), unless the Finanzamt asks for a declaration (§ 149 Abs. 1 S. 2 AO). Invoice note "steuerfreier Kleinunternehmer" (BMF 18 Mar 2025).

**Income tax is unaffected.** Kleinunternehmer status is a VAT rule. Profit is still taxed (EÜR, § 4 Abs. 3 EStG) and a freelancer/trade classification still applies.

**Because there is no input VAT:** expenses are deductible at gross cost, and the EUR 800 GWG test (§ 6 Abs. 2 EStG: cost minus input VAT) therefore runs on the gross price.

**Switch decisions (advise with numbers).**
- Waive when customers are VAT-registered businesses and you have large VAT-bearing purchases (equipment, software): you recover input VAT and customers don't care about the VAT.
- Stay when customers are consumers: 19% VAT would be a price increase or a margin cut.
- BMF 10 Nov 2025: input VAT on purchases made before the switch to Regelbesteuerung is not deductible for the period before the switch, even if the switch is likely.

**Cross-border.** Which invoicing rules apply to EU customers and the obligation to obtain a VAT ID: read UStAE 19.x in `references/bmf/ustae-aktuell.txt` before advising (§ 19 Abs. 1 S. 2 disapplies parts of § 14a). E-invoices: Kleinunternehmer may keep issuing other formats (§ 34a UStDV) but must be able to receive them.

**Typical traps.** Mixing gross/net when testing the limits; forgetting that crossing EUR 100,000 mid-year turns the very invoice that crosses the line into a taxed one; mistaking a waived status for a free choice every year (5-year lock).
