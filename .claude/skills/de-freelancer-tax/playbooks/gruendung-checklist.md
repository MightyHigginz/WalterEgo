# Start-up checklist (freelancer or trade)

1. Classify: Freiberufler (§ 18 EStG) or Gewerbe. Doubt -> binding ruling (§ 89 AO) or ask the Finanzamt before starting.
2. Register: Gewerbe at the municipality if trade; Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung via ELSTER for both, within one month of starting (§ 138 Abs. 4 AO, statute checked).
3. In the questionnaire: expected profit for the start year and next (drives advance payments), EÜR, VAT choice (Kleinunternehmer vs. Regelbesteuerung; waiver binds 5 years), Ist-Versteuerung request, VAT ID if EU business.
4. Open a separate business bank account; keep every receipt (retention 8 years for vouchers, 10 for books, § 147 AO).
5. Insurance: statutory/private health, pension strategy (Basisrente), liability insurance.
6. Invoices: legal content (§ 14 UStG), e-invoice capability (receive since 2025, issue from 2027/2028).
7. Set aside a tax reserve (about 25-35% of profit as default until the first assessment).
8. Advance payments: track the notice (Vorauszahlungsbescheid) and the 10 Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec dates.
9. Founders' VAT: monthly returns in the start year and the next, with the 2021-2026 estimation rule (§ 18 Abs. 2 UStG).
10. First year: file income tax return (due 31 Jul of the following year, or end of Feb of the second following year via an advisor).
