# GmbH / UG (haftungsbeschränkt)

**Tax stack (statute checked).** Corporation tax 15% for 2025-2027; falling by one point per year to 14% (2028), 13% (2029), 12% (2030), 11% (2031), 10% (2032 onwards) (§ 23 KStG). Plus solidarity surcharge 5.5% of corporation tax, plus trade tax (3.5% x Hebesatz; no EUR 24,500 allowance for corporations).

**Paying out.** Salary to the managing director (deductible for the company, income for the person; check appropriateness: **verdeckte Gewinnausschüttung** risk in related-party deals, § 8 Abs. 3 KStG). Dividends: 25% flat Abgeltungsteuer (§ 32d EStG) or optional Teileinkünfteverfahren (60% taxable, § 3 Nr. 40 EStG) for shareholders with 25%/1% active participation - an application is needed.

**Formation.** Notarised articles and register entry (GmbHG, text in `statutes/gmbhg.md`); UG capital from EUR 1 but must retain 25% of annual profit; GmbH minimum EUR 25,000.

**When it pays.** Roughly when profit is retained in the company (reinvestment, pension provisions) and the owner's personal marginal rate would be high. If the whole profit is distributed, the combined burden is about 30% corporate plus 25% on dividends (~47%), more than a sole proprietorship in many bands. Run a numeric comparison; do not give a verdict without profit, payout and trade tax rate.

**Compliance.** Double-entry accounts, annual accounts and disclosure, trade tax return, corporation tax return, VAT as usual, payroll for the managing director, liability risks (insolvency filing duties, GmbHG).
