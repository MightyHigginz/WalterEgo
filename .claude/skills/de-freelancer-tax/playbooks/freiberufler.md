# Freiberufler (§ 18 EStG)

**Who.** Self-employed scientific, artistic, literary, teaching or educational work, and the catalog professions (doctors, lawyers, notaries, engineers, architects, auditors, tax advisors, translators, journalists ...) plus "similar professions" (§ 18 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 EStG, statute text checked).

**Tax features.**
- No trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) and no commercial registration; register with the Finanzamt via ELSTER (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung).
- Profit by cash-basis EÜR (§ 4 Abs. 3 EStG) - no bookkeeping duty. Voluntary balance-sheet accounting is possible but can cost the Ist-Versteuerung (see BFH note in research digest).
- VAT: regular 19%/7% unless Kleinunternehmer. Ist-Versteuerung (taxed on payment) is available to freelancers regardless of turnover (§ 20 Nr. 3 UStG, application required).
- Mixed activity: a sole freelancer with a separate trade-like activity is assessed separately for each (activities must be separable, with separate accounts). In a partnership (GbR, PartG, KG) one commercial activity makes all of the partnership's income commercial (**Abfärbung**, § 15 Abs. 3 Nr. 1 EStG, statute checked): split into separate entities.
- Employees: a freelancer can employ trained staff and remain freelancer if they lead and supervise personally (§ 18 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 S. 3 EStG).

**IT/software.** Freelance only if engineer-like or truly similar to a catalog profession plus matching training or proven expertise; otherwise trade. When uncertain recommend a binding ruling (**verbindliche Auskunft**, § 89 AO) before invoicing.

**Social-law checks.** Single main client >5/6 of revenue and no employees -> pension insurance risk (§ 2 SGB VI); consider Statusfeststellung (§ 7a SGB IV). Artists/publicists -> Künstlersozialkasse.

**Standard planning list.** EÜR hygiene; Homeoffice-Pauschale or Arbeitszimmer (Module D); IAB § 7g; degressive AfA 30% (§ 7 Abs. 2); e-vehicle 75% year-one write-off (§ 7 Abs. 2a, assets bought 1 Jul 2025 - 31 Dec 2027, no special depreciation); Basisrente; health insurance; advance payments adjusted to real profit; Ist-Versteuerung to protect cash flow.
