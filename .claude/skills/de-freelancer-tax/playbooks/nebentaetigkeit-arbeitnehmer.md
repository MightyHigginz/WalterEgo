# Employees with side income

**Filing duty.** Employees must file an income tax return if side income that is not subject to wage tax exceeds EUR 410 per year (§ 46 Abs. 2 Nr. 1 EStG, statute checked). Below it no filing duty on that ground; above it the whole side profit counts.

**Tax-free allowances (statute checked).** Übungsleiterpauschale EUR 3,300/year (§ 3 Nr. 26 EStG: trainers, instructors, educators, carers, certain artistic work, for non-profit or public bodies) and Ehrenamtspauschale EUR 960/year (§ 3 Nr. 26a). They cannot be combined on the same income and exclude expense deduction on the same tax-free part.

**Side business.** Self-employed side job is taxed like any other: EÜR, Kleinunternehmer possible, advance payments possible, Gewerbe or Freiberufler classification applies. Employer approval may be required by contract.

**Social insurance.** Statutory health insurance as an employee covers the side income; as a freelancer the main-job insurance may require the side income to stay below "hauptberuflich selbständig" thresholds (check KV/SV rules). Do not advise on the Beitragsrecht beyond flagging.

**Tax class.** Steuerklasse only affects wage-tax withholding, not final tax. Side profit raises the final tax via progression; adjust advance payments to avoid a big back-payment.

**Optimisation.** Employee costs: Werbungskosten (§ 9 EStG) and the home office rules (Nr. 6b/6c apply to both). Basisrente is available to employees too (reduced by statutory pension contributions).
