# Capital income, shares, crypto and private sales

**Capital income (§ 20 EStG).** Flat tax 25% (§ 32d Abs. 1) plus solidarity surcharge and church tax; banks withhold it. Saver's allowance (Sparer-Pauschbetrag) EUR 1,000 single / EUR 2,000 joint (§ 20 Abs. 9, statute checked) - give the bank an exemption order (Freistellungsauftrag). Option: assessment at the personal rate if lower (Günstigerprüfung).

**Private sales (§ 23 EStG, statute checked).** Gain on assets held up to 1 year (other assets; property: 10 years) is taxable; tax-free if total gain in the year is under EUR 1,000 (Freigrenze, not an allowance: at EUR 1,000 or more the entire amount is taxed). Items of daily use are excluded. Crypto: treat as other assets (BMF letter on virtual currencies applies, not yet loaded); hold more than 1 year for tax-free sale; staking/lending can extend the holding period or create other income.

**Self-employed with investments.** Capital income is separate from business profit. Holding shares through a GmbH changes the tax logic (see gmbh-ug.md).

**Disclosure.** Capital gains abroad or crypto on foreign exchanges must be declared; foreign accounts are subject to reporting. Recommend professional help for complex cases.
